# How 3D Printing Is Reshaping North American Housing

3D printing — or additive manufacturing — is reshaping housing construction across North America by automating selected construction activities, improving material efficiency, and reducing dependence on conventional building processes. Using techniques like concrete extrusion and powder bonding with concrete, plastics, metals, and composites, builders are tackling labor constraints and unlocking new approaches to residential design. Companies such as ICON, Mighty Buildings, Apis Cor, SQ4D, PERI SE, and ALQUIST are active in this space, per MarkNtel Advisors research, though challenges like equipment costs, building codes, and financing remain.
